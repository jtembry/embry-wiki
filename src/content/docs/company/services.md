## An AI system for your office

An off-the-shelf chatbot answers from the public internet and can make things up. This one answers from what your business has learned over the years, and your clients' data stays private.

- Every email, text and scan read and filed where it belongs, with the noise archived and replies drafted.
- Paper and handwriting turned into searchable text.
- Your documents indexed in place, with access rules that match your office.
- An assistant that takes plain-English questions from your staff and shows its sources every time (retrieval-augmented generation: it looks up the matching passages first, then answers from them and cites them).
- [HomeADE](/embry-wiki/homeade/) on the computer that holds the work.
- Runs on Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, or an open-source model.

Then four weeks of training beside your staff on real work. [How the training works](https://embry.dev/knowledge-assistants/ai-adoption).

## Custom builds

When the office needs a piece the system does not have, such as a customer portal, a scheduling tool, or a phone app for the crew, it is built to plug into the same system, with your records kept in a place you own. A working prototype within days, then changes together until it fits. You own the code. [LightScan](/embry-wiki/lightscan/) and the [Flourish Financial Group site](/embry-wiki/flourish/) are two examples.
