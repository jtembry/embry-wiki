Embry Development, LLC is JT Embry's studio in the Louisville metropolitan area, formed in 2026. When you email, the person who answers is the person who does the work.

JT spent ten years building finance and healthcare software, first as an engineer and then as a lead: a SaaS product from an empty repository, a video portal for hospitals, a platform moved to a new cloud, and the AI tooling that raised one team's output by 65 percent. Before that: a bank's help desk, LiDAR data for a self-driving program, and a degree in English literature.

The studio builds AI systems for small offices, keeps the records on a computer the office owns, and trains the staff. When the office needs a piece that does not exist, it builds that too. FieldADE and HomeADE are the system JT runs his own work on, shipped as products.
