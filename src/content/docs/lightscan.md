LightScan is a custom build for one foundation repair company. It is their app and not a public download; it is here because it shows the kind of tool Embry Development builds.

## What it does

The estimator walks the house with an iPhone. The phone's laser scanner draws the rooms and the wall lengths while they are still inside. A heat map marks the high and low spots in the floor. The same scan becomes a customer report and a model the crew can 3D print.

The first version was on their phone a day after they described the problem.

## A build like this

Tools like LightScan are built around the way a crew already works: one job, one phone, the record written while the work happens. [Custom builds](https://embry.dev/software) on embry.dev says how a project like that runs, and [one email](mailto:joel@embry.dev?subject=A%20phone%20app%20for%20my%20crew) starts it.
