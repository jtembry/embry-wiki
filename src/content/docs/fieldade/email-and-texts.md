---
title: Email and texts
description: Every inbox mail and unread text as a row with the same actions. Open, archive, delete, triage, add to calendar, respond with AI, send to an agent.
---

**Email** shows every message still in your inboxes (read or not). **Messages** shows every unread text. Both are plain feeds from the Mac; opening the section checks the feed at once, with a "Checking mail…" line while it waits. There is no timer: rows arrive by live push.

## Actions on a row

Every row has the same set, on the swipes and in the long-press menu.

- **Open** shows the thread in the app. Email renders the sender's own HTML with pictures; a text thread shows bubbles with the sender's name in a group and pictures in line. Links open in Edge or Safari.
- **Archive** and **Delete** act on the account at once; a toast gives you five seconds to undo.
- **Triage** files the thread as an Ingest note under a project, after the exact-plan confirm described in [Ingest](/embry-wiki/fieldade/ingest/).
- **Add to Calendar** shows on a row that names a date. It reads the whole thread, shows you the title, day, time and place, and checks the phone's calendars for a clash ("Already on your calendar" or "Calendar conflict", with Add anyway). A phone add can be undone from the Ingest Log.
- **Respond with AI** drafts a reply from the whole thread. For email you pick the From account (personal or business), edit, attach files or photos, and **Send** from the Mac. The email stays in the inbox. For a text, the draft opens in Messages with the thread filled in; you send it.
- **Send to AI** opens a chat named after the row with the whole thread in front of the agent, and asks what it is and what to do. Nothing is sent, archived or filed until you say so.
- **Unsubscribe** on a list mail sends the one-click unsubscribe, blocks the sender and trashes the mail.

Group texts show who sent each line and a strip of recent pictures under the row. Tap a picture to see it full size.

## Sent

The Email section has an **Inbox | Sent** switch. Sent lists the last thirty from each account, including mail sent from the business address, with Open and Send to AI.

## Which accounts

Settings → Choose sources picks the machine whose mail and texts you see, and **Inboxes** lists the accounts on that machine. Add one with its address and an app password; remove one with a swipe. Outlook and Hotmail are refused because they have no app passwords.
