## Widgets

On iOS 26 or later, FieldADE offers a small, medium and large widget for the chat list (each chat's dot and one line) and one per Today section: Today, Email, Messages, Calendar, Ingest, Projects, Areas, MOCs, Skills, Work Log, Ingest Log, Vault. They refresh by push when anything changes on the machine and keep the last copy when the machine is away. Tap one to open that section. Private scans show only as "Private scan".

## Share sheet

Share from any app and pick **FieldADE**. It takes up to 20 photos, videos or files, one web link, or text. Each goes to the machine that feeds your Today screen and lands in Ingest as a note with the file embedded. The card says Saved and closes. If the machine is away or the connection is down, the item waits on the phone and goes out the next time you open FieldADE.

## CarPlay

In the car FieldADE is a voice-only app. Connecting starts a call with Grok at once; Grok can ask the chat's agent on the Mac anything ("what's due", "read my texts", "mark that done") and reads the answer back. Nothing is shown on the car screen but the call state and a Hang up button. Unplugging hangs up. CarPlay needs Apple's approval for the app, which is pending.

## Handoff

A chat or a section open on the phone shows up as a Handoff activity on the Mac, and the other way round, with the half-typed draft carried over.
