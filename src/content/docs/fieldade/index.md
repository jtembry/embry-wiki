FieldADE is the iPhone app for [HomeADE](/embry-wiki/homeade/), the desk on your computer where your agents work. From the phone you see what is due, read mail and texts, file what just came in under a project with a swipe, and start a chat that already knows the project. A file edit or a command waits until you allow it.

**Requirements.** An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. A Mac running HomeADE for the full system, or nothing at all in [phone-only mode](/embry-wiki/fieldade/settings/#phone-only). Windows HomeADE cannot pair a phone yet.

**Get it.** [FieldADE on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fieldade/id6828288883) · [Release notes](/embry-wiki/fieldade/release-notes/) · [Privacy policy](https://embry.dev/fieldade/privacy)

## The screens

| Screen | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Today](/embry-wiki/fieldade/today-screen/) | What is due, what is happening, follow-ups, projects gone quiet. The root of the app. |
| Email, Texts | Your inboxes and unread texts as rows with the same five actions. See [Email and texts](/embry-wiki/fieldade/email-and-texts/). |
| Calendar | Your Google and iCloud calendars, straight from the phone, plus follow-ups. |
| [Ingest](/embry-wiki/fieldade/ingest/) | The inbox of the notes: scans, pasted notes, shared files, recordings, each with a proposed home. |
| Projects, Areas | Cards with the phase and next action of each, and the open to-dos under them. |
| [Agents](/embry-wiki/fieldade/chats/) | Every chat on every machine, each assistant's limit, open terminals. |
| More | Skills, Rules, Hooks, Connectors, Scheduled, Tickets, Vault search, Ingest Log, Work Log. Drag any of them onto the bar. |

## Two looks

Settings → Look switches between **Professional** (iOS system colors) and **DOS** (green phosphor on black). Everything else is the same.
