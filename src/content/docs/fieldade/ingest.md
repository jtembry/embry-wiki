title: Ingest
description: File a scan, a note, a shared file or a recording under the right project with one swipe. The plan is shown before anything moves.
---

Ingest is the inbox of your notes, the folder named `0 Ingest` in the vault. Every row is one item waiting to be filed. The badge on the tile counts them.

## Filing an item

Swipe right on a row and tap **Triage**. A confirm shows exactly what will happen: the kind (to-do, note, concept, quote, discard), the project or area it will go under, and the wording. Tap to run it. The plan is made ahead of time for every new row, so the confirm appears at once; typing a note of your own in the box re-plans with it.

A to-do or a discard is done in about a second with no model. A note, concept or quote runs an agent on the Mac. A failed triage comes back on the row as "Triage failed · Retry".

**Triage all** at the bottom of the list files everything with a plan, after one confirm.

## Other actions on a row

- **AI** (right swipe, or Send to AI in the long-press menu): opens a chat named after the note with the whole note in front of the agent, and asks "What is this, and what should we do with it?". Nothing is filed until you say so.
- **Delete**: trash, with a five-second Undo.
- **Done** on a reminder capture completes the linked Apple Reminder and trashes the note.
- **Dream** on a memory-review note opens a sheet of proposals to tick, then applies the ticked ones in a chat.

## Scans

A scanned document shows up with a proposed folder and name. **File** opens that name for editing and files the PDF into your Documents under it. **View scan** shows the pages in the app. A scan that holds a Social Security number, a card or bank account number, a passport or ID number is flagged **private** before its text enters the vault: File puts the PDF in a fenced private folder with no model involved, and **Index anyway** sends it back to the normal path. Every scan's File sheet has **File private** too.

A scanned **business card** adds a **Contact** action that opens the iPhone's New Contact card filled in. Nothing is saved until you tap Done.

## Loose files and Transcribe

A file in the Ingest folder that is not a note shows as a row with its kind and size. An audio or video file has **Transcribe**: pick an engine (the built-in one downloads its speech model once, about the size it names) and a model for the summary. The recording is transcribed on the Mac, the row shows progress with Cancel, and a note with a summary, key points, decisions and action items appears in Ingest with the recording embedded. Then you are offered the normal triage.

## New note

The box at the top of Ingest writes a note straight into the folder. Tap and hold to paste a photo or screenshot; thumbnails show above the box. Offline, the phone writes the note into its own copy of the vault.

## The share sheet

Share a photo, a file, a link or text from any app to FieldADE and it lands in Ingest. See [Widgets, share sheet, CarPlay](/embry-wiki/fieldade/widgets-share-carplay/).
