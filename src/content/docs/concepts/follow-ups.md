---
title: Follow-ups
description: A check an agent schedules for later shows on the chat, on Today, on the calendar, and can copy to Apple Reminders.
---

When an agent says "wait a day, then look at the log", it does not have to remember. It leaves a **follow-up**: a message addressed to its own chat at a set time. At that time the message is sent into the chat and the agent does the check without anyone asking.

## Where you see it

- **The chat row** shows a clock with the weekday, time and text of the next one.
- **Today** has a Follow-ups block of every pending check across your machines.
- **The calendar** lists each one under its day; checks past the synced week sit under Later follow-ups.
- **Scheduled** (under Harness, or More on the phone) lists them with every other timed job on the machine, with a Cancel button.
- A push arrives when one fires, and tapping it opens the chat. If that chat has since been closed, the tap opens Today instead of some other chat.

## Scheduling your own

You can schedule a message of your own to a chat from the phone the same way; it shows the same clock.

## Closing the chat

A follow-up outlives its chat, but it never reopens a chat on its own. Until its time it is listed as "(closed)" on Today and the calendar, and can be cancelled there. When its time comes nothing is sent: a push says "Follow-up due" and Today shows it under **Follow-ups due** with two buttons:

- **Run** reopens that conversation and sends the check into it, then opens the chat. If the conversation is gone from the machine, or open in a terminal right now, it says so and the row stays.
- **Dismiss** drops it. Nothing is sent.

Nothing appears in your chat list unless you tap Run. A follow-up also waits this way if its chat is still open but has moved on to a different conversation. Deleting a chat deletes its follow-ups.

## Apple Reminders

On the phone, Settings → Follow-ups → **Also add to Apple Reminders** mirrors every pending check into a FieldADE list in Reminders, due at its time, with no alarm of its own (the push is the alert). Ticking or deleting the reminder cancels the check; a check that fires or is cancelled ticks its reminder.
