Flourish Financial Group is a website built for an accredited financial counselor. It is her site. It is here because it shows the kind of website Embry Development builds.

## What it does

A visitor reads what she offers and books a free consult from the home page. The pages are static, they load fast, and hosting costs nothing a month.

The site went live about a month after the first conversation.

## She updates it herself

The site lives in her own GitHub account, with a written guide. She changes a paragraph, an hour, or a photo herself.
