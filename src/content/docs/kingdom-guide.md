Kingdom Guide plans a day at Magic Kingdom. Pick the rides; the app puts them in walking order and estimates when you will reach each one, using the posted waits. It began as a page for one family's trip and is headed for the App Store.

## A day you can walk

The app orders the rides by the walk between them and shows the arrival time at each. The map draws the route, names the next stop, and shows your blue dot when you allow location. Change the list and the plan re-orders.

## Disability Access Service

For a family who uses the Disability Access Service, the plan says which ride to book next. Enter the return time from the Disney app and the phone notifies you when it comes up.

## Meals and the lock screen

Meal reminders are optional: 45 minutes ahead for quick-service lunch and dinner, 15 minutes ahead for snacks and table service. On the trip day the lock screen shows the next stop, its posted wait, and the return you hold. It ends when the day is done.

## Offline

The planner is built into the app. It keeps the last waits, hours and trip changes it fetched, and changes you make offline go out when the signal returns.

## Privacy

No account, no tracking. Location stays on the phone. The [privacy policy](https://embry.dev/kingdom-guide/privacy) lists what leaves it; [support](https://embry.dev/kingdom-guide/support) answers the usual questions.
